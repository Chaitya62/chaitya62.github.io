I build systems that scale, teams that execute, and products that actually ship.

I'm Chaitya Shah — CTO and Co-founder at [Kyma Lifestyle](https://kymalifestyle.com), where we're building an AI concierge that helps banks transform their loyalty programs. Think personalized, white-glove experiences for cardholders over WhatsApp and iMessage — discovery, booking, post-purchase support, all handled by an agentic system that actually gets things done.

Before Kyma, I spent nearly 7 years at BrowserStack leading engineering work across large-scale distributed systems — re-architecting core infrastructure, scaling critical services, and driving end-to-end ownership on products used by millions of developers.

I operate at the intersection of systems thinking, product instinct, and raw execution. I care about solving real problems, not just writing code. Whether it's breaking monoliths into scalable services or designing agentic AI architectures, I focus on things that hold up under pressure.

Lately I've been deep in agentic systems, long-term memory architectures, and AI-driven products for both developers and consumers. I write about these ideas here, along with lessons on engineering, ownership, and building in the age of AI.

If you're building something ambitious or thinking hard about the future of software, [say hi](mailto:chaitya62@gmail.com).
